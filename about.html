About TETUCO Limited

TETUCO Limited is an engineering, construction and contracting company providing reliable technical and support services to clients across different sectors.

Our operations cover mechanical engineering, pipeline construction, electrical and instrumentation services, welding and fabrication, tubing, catering, equipment hiring and leasing, quarry and granite supply, and general contracting.

We are committed to providing practical solutions that meet project requirements while maintaining professionalism, quality workmanship and dependable service delivery.
